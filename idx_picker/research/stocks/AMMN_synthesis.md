## The headline: my screener was badly wrong, and the research says so

**The screener's IV of IDR 1,127 (base 136, bull 743) is a methodology artifact.**
A proper sum-of-the-parts mine NPV lands at **~IDR 3,900** — the screener understated
intrinsic value by roughly **4.5x**. This is the largest single screener error the project
has produced, and it is a *structural* failure, not a data error:

- It anchored to trailing EBIT during a **company-disclosed trough** — the Batu Hijau
  Phase 7→8 grade transition (copper grade bottomed at **0.31% Cu**) plus concurrent
  smelter-ramp losses.
- It **cannot see an orebody**. 705Mt of Batu Hijau reserves and a feasibility-complete
  Elang (2,526Mt, 17.78 Blb Cu, 26.44 Moz Au) contribute nothing to a trailing multiple.

The trough is already reversing on the company's own numbers: **Q1 2026 net income
USD 163m at a 20% margin against a USD 138m loss in Q1 2025**, EBITDA margin swinging
from −63% to **+63%**, with copper grade up 0.31%→**0.53%** and gold grade 0.17→**0.54 g/t**.

**This is the same blind spot that appeared at LSIP and INDY, in its third costume:** the
screener has no concept of a finite-life or phased asset. At LSIP it missed a replanting
deficit; at INDY a 2033 licence wall; here a disclosed grade cycle. Twice it made a
company look *cheaper* than it was. Here it made one look far more expensive.

## Resolving the disagreement

The spread is real: FAIRLY VALUED against REJECT at 78/100 concern. Both survive scrutiny
because they answer different questions.

**The valuation agent kept itself honest in both directions**, which is why I weight it
heavily. Having demolished the screener, it did not then declare a bargain: reconstructed
**TTM EV/EBIT is still ~21–25x**, and annualising the best quarter versus the latest swings
the multiple from ~9x to ~19x. **There is no stable normalised multiple yet.** Its base case
of 3,900 uses conservative long-run prices (Cu $4.50/lb, Au $3,200/oz) against spot near
records (~$6.60/lb, ~$4,325/oz) — so at *today's* metal prices the asset is worth more, and
the price already embeds much of that.

**The two supposed catalysts are weaker than the narrative**, and the agents caught both:

1. **The smelter is a defensive necessity, not a profit centre.** The business agent read
   the 900kt smelter as a moat — it converts Indonesia's January 2025 concentrate export
   ban from a threat into an advantage, which is real. But the skeptic found **global copper
   treatment and refining charges have gone negative** — smelters *paying* to process ore
   amid a capacity glut. Both hold: the smelter protects the right to sell; it does not
   currently earn a downstream margin. Any re-rating premised on "downstream margin
   capture" is unsupported today.
2. **Elang is a bigger deposit, not a better one.** Grade is **no better than Batu Hijau's**,
   it needs ~USD 3bn from 2028, and it is pre-permitting. That is an option, not an asset —
   and it arrives *before* the smelter debt meaningfully amortises.

**Where the skeptic is decisive:** FY2025 was AMMN's **worst year since IPO on nearly every
axis** — six of nine Piotroski criteria failing, total debt up 3.5x in three years to
USD 6.5bn, operating cash flow of **−USD 475m against +USD 249m net income**. Rising
leverage and shrinking margins **in the same year the market paid up to 3.3x book**.

Three further facts belong in front of any buyer:
- **Insiders sold large blocks in Aug–Sep 2025, clustered at the 52-week high**, just before
  a ~49% decline.
- **Management explicitly declines to guide FY2026 cathode and refined-gold production** —
  it will not commit to the numbers the bull case needs.
- **MSCI deleted AMMN on 2026-05-29** on its 18.7% free float, causing a −14.86% single-day
  fall. A demonstrated, fundamentals-independent tail risk.

## 1. Investment classification

**Cyclical, high-quality asset at a fair-to-full price.** Not a value trap — the asset is
genuinely world-class. Not a bargain — the market knows it.

## 2. One-line thesis

> The market is pricing AMMN as a recovering world-class copper-gold miner and is roughly
> right; my screener priced it off the worst grade quarter of the mine plan and was wrong
> by 4.5x — but correcting that error produces a fair value *below* today's price, not
> above it.

## 3. Why does it look expensive?

- PE 41.2, PB 3.29, EV/EBIT 19.1 (~21–25x reconstructed) on a cyclical miner.
- F-Score 3, six of nine signals failing.
- FCF yield −7% on a IDR 324tn market cap; net debt/equity 1.18 into BI tightening at 5.75%.
- ~42% rally since the June 2026 sell-side initiation.

## 4. What is the market missing?

**Very little.** The genuine asset quality is real and confirmed from primary sources:
**negative C1 cash cost** — minus USD 3.37/lb in 2024, minus USD 0.54/lb in 2025 — because
gold and silver by-product credits more than cover the cost of producing copper. That is
top-of-industry. But at 3.3x book the market is already paying for it.

## 5. What changes?

- **Grade recovery** — real, dated, and already in the Q1 2026 print. Partly priced.
- **Copper supply shock** — Freeport's Grasberg force majeure cuts 2026 output ~32% and
  pushes refined copper into deficit. Genuine, exogenous, and *not* AMMN-specific.
- Freeport concentrate-supply talks with AMMN: unresolved and two-sided.

## 6–8. Macro / Industry / Micro

**Macro TAILWIND (66/100)** — copper at or near records (LME USD 13,411/t, 7 Aug 2026),
gold ~USD 4,350 spot (31.9% of Q1 revenue). **Industry TAILWIND on price, HEADWIND on
smelting** — negative TC/RCs. **Micro RECOVERING** — grades and margins inflecting, but
leverage rising and no production guidance.

## 9. Fundamental earnings engine

Copper and gold tonnes × grade × price, less cash costs that are *negative* after by-product
credits. Grade phase is the swing factor; copper price dominates value, gold underpins the
cost advantage.

## 10. Catalyst

**MODERATE (62/100)** — the strongest of any name reviewed. The difference from CTRA, LSIP
and INDY is that AMMN's catalyst is **already occurring and dated**, not hoped for. The
problem is that it is also already partly paid for.

## 12. Downside

Not a wipeout — the asset is real and the cost position is the best in this review. But
bear 1,850 is **−59%**, driven by metal prices normalising while USD 6.5bn of debt and a
USD 3bn Elang programme remain. Leverage converts a commodity correction into an equity
drawdown.

## 13. Thesis-breaker (what would make it a buy)

1. A price near **IDR 2,900–3,100** — a 20–25% discount to the conservative-price NPV.
2. Verified positive smelter margin (requires TC/RCs to recover from negative).
3. A **funded, non-dilutive** Elang capex plan.
4. Management issuing FY2026 downstream production guidance.

## 14. Research gaps

Flagged rather than guessed: debt maturity schedule and covenants (PDF font-encoded,
defeated fetch); the NI-vs-CFO accrual gap; year-by-year mine schedule beyond FY2026;
company-confirmed Elang capex (a ~USD 3bn broker estimate was used); related-party
disclosures; AMMN-specific WACC.

## Verdict

**WATCH — upgraded from the screener's SKIP, but still no position at IDR 4,470.**

This is the most valuable result the system has produced, because **it caught my own
screener being badly wrong in the direction that matters least to a value investor —
wrongly rejecting a great asset.** A screen that only ever errs toward caution still costs
you every opportunity it discards for the wrong reason, and here it discarded the best
business in the review on an intrinsic value 4.5x too low.

The honest conclusion is uncomfortable for both sides: **the screener's rejection was
wrong, and its conclusion was right.** Correcting the methodology moves fair value from
1,127 to 3,900 — and 3,900 is still **below** the 4,470 price.

**Actionable: no position. Set an alert at IDR 3,100.** At that level the conservative-price
NPV provides a genuine margin of safety, and you would be buying a negative-cash-cost
copper-gold asset rather than a commodity-price forecast. Treat any position as **satellite,
not core** — 18.7% free float and a demonstrated −14.86% index-flow day make this
untradeable in size.

## Resolving the disagreement

Unusually, **all five agents converged**, and three of them independently reached the same
conclusion from different documents. The disagreement worth recording is that the Thesis
Killer's assigned kill *failed* and it said so.

**The hypothesised kill — "growth capex will eat the free cash flow" — is wrong.** 2026
capex is USD 10–20m of explicitly "protection" capex, running at **8.8% of depreciation**
(USD 4.08m against USD 46.56m). ESSA **suspended its SAF project on 27 July 2026**. There
is no second ammonia plant and **no soda ash venture at all** — a premise this project
introduced into the briefs and which the agents correctly refuted. The free cash flow is
genuinely uncommitted.

**The real thesis-breaker is contractual, and it is not in any published model.** Every
commercial agreement underpinning the company expires within about 16 months:

| Contract | Counterparty | Expiry |
|---|---|---|
| Feed-gas GSPA (88.39% of raw-material cost) | JOB Pertamina-Medco Tomori | Dec 2027 |
| Ammonia offtake (89.88% of revenue) | Genesis Corporation (common control) | 3 Dec 2027 |
| LPG feedstock | Pertamina EP | 31 Dec 2027 |
| LPG sales | Pertamina Patra Niaga | 31 Jul 2027 |

Pertamina takes 50% and operatorship of Senoro-Toili from 4 December 2027. ESSA's own
Note 32 states termination "could result in the discontinuation of the Company's
business." Renegotiation is targeted for end-2026.

**The single most important finding cuts *for* the company and reframes everything.** The
gas price is **contractually indexed to the ammonia price**. That is why a +70% realised
price move produced only a 27.7% → 35.2% gross-margin expansion, why EBIT margin held at
22–27% through a 60% revenue collapse, and why ESSA stayed profitable through the 2023–25
downcycle. ESSA is a **damped-spread business, not a leveraged ammonia bet** — materially
better than the "Cyclical" label implies. It is also precisely why the *expiring contract*,
not the ammonia price, is the thesis-breaker: the indexation is the asset, and it is up
for renegotiation.

**Four screener defects were found and have been fixed universe-wide.** Enterprise value
(Yahoo recognised IDR 345bn of a IDR 2,945bn net cash pile — 557 of 956 tickers affected);
payout ratio (0.2023 reported against a true 125% — 49 rows affected); attributable net
income (consolidated EPS 54.67 vs attributable 41.9, the 30% PAU minority); and the
earlier EBIT/EBITDA contamination. ESSA now reads EV/EBIT 5.0 and payout 0.95.

## 1. Investment classification

**Cyclical with a damped spread, carrying an unpriced binary contract-renewal event.**
Not a compounder — utilisation is already 121% of nameplate, so there is no volume
headroom. Not deep value — the balance sheet is a fortress but the equity is not
statistically cheap once the dividend and EV are corrected.

## 2. One-line thesis

> The market is pricing ESSA as an ordinary ammonia cyclical on a cheap multiple, but it
> is actually a damped-spread utility whose entire economics rest on one gas contract —
> and that contract, covering 88% of the cost base, resets on unknown terms in 16 months.

## 3. Why is it cheap?

- Revenue fell at a −26% CAGR over three years (a price cycle, now inflecting).
- Every material contract expires in 2027 with no renewal disclosed.
- ~90% single-customer concentration in a **related party under common control**, at
  prices the company itself flags may not be arm's-length.
- ~6% of PAU EBITDA leaks to shareholder affiliates; related-party management fees run
  ~29x total board compensation.
- The market sold Rp 655 → Rp 610 *despite* H1 profit doubling — it is pricing this
  deliberately, not overlooking it.

## 4. What is the market missing?

Genuinely little on the downside. What may be underappreciated is the **indexation** —
ESSA is structurally more defensive than its sector label. But that is an argument about
*quality*, not about *price*.

## 5. What changes?

Only the gas renegotiation, targeted end-2026. It is **binary with an unknown sign**:
preserved indexation re-rates the business; market-linked pricing destroys the model.

## 6–8. Macro / Industry / Micro

**Macro NEUTRAL (55/100)** — naturally long USD (net non-USD exposure USD 0.97m, zero FX
debt), so a weak IDR flatters translated earnings; BI tightening cuts the other way.
**Industry HEADWIND** — Tampa CFR settled ~USD 487/MT in August 2026 after a USD 110 July
drop, now *below* ESSA's H1 realised ASP of USD 531/MT; the Hormuz risk premium is being
negotiated away and China has relaxed urea exports.
**Micro MIXED** — H1 2026 revenue USD 186m (+36%), net profit USD 30m (+103%), but
**production fell 8% to 321kt** and Q2 halved sequentially on a 34–48 day turnaround
(completed 8 June 2026).

## 9. Fundamental earnings engine

Ammonia price × volume, less gas cost that moves *with* the ammonia price. Only ~66% of
any ammonia move reaches the spread. Volume is capped — utilisation is already 121% of
nameplate.

## 10. Catalyst

**WEAK (35/100).** The price catalyst has already fired and is reversing. SAF suspended.
Blue ammonia is pre-FID awaiting IMO with no offtake and economics needing a 14% price
premium against an observed 6–12%. No soda ash project exists.

## 12. Downside

Not solvency — genuinely debt-free (USD 134.5k of leases against USD 176.8m of cash and
bonds; USD 92.2m repaid in FY2025), accruals honest (CFO/NI 1.70x reconciling exactly to
depreciation). The downside is the **2027 contract cliff**, which is not a price risk but
an existence-of-business risk by the company's own disclosure.

## 13. Thesis-breaker

1. A post-2027 gas agreement **without** ammonia indexation.
2. FID on blue ammonia Phase 2 or SAF — removes ~IDR 171/share of net cash.
3. Any change to the Genesis offtake terms.
4. Ammonia below ~USD 350/t sustained.

## 14. Research gaps

- Terms under negotiation for the post-2027 GSPA — the single most valuable unknown.
- Whether Genesis pricing is arm's-length; no independent verification is disclosed.
- Whether the FY2026 dividend reverts to a covered payout (guided Rp 42–56 against Rp 52).

## Verdict

**WATCH — confirmed, not upgraded.** The screener's WATCH was the right call for the wrong
reasons: it arrived there via a 10% MOS built on a wrong EV, a wrong payout ratio and a
consolidated EPS. Corrected, the MOS is ~21% — better than the screener thought — but the
company carries a binary contract event the screener cannot see at all, and its Safety
Score of 95.6 with zero red flags is **structurally blind to it**.

Three agents independently set a buy limit in a tight band: **IDR 500 (Agent 5), 581
(Agent 2), ~500–525 (Agent 5 bear)**.

**Actionable: no position at 610–655. Limit at IDR 560, sized as a satellite, not a core
holding. Alert on any disclosure of the post-2027 gas agreement — if indexation survives,
this re-rates and the quality score goes to ~65–68.**

## Resolving the disagreement

There was little to resolve — all five converged — but the *route* matters, because the
bull case was given a fair hearing and failed on evidence rather than on prejudice.

**The bull case was specific and testable:** INDY is a holding company at 0.65x book,
owning 91% of Kideco, which was reportedly being courted at a **>USD 1bn** valuation
against INDY's entire market cap of ~USD 779m. If true, the parts exceed the whole.

**Three findings killed it, each independently sufficient.**

1. **The ventures are not a free option — they are actively destroying value.** In Q1 2026
   Kideco alone earned **USD 42.4m** of net profit while INDY's *consolidated* net profit
   was **USD 7m**. The non-Kideco ventures plus holdco costs consumed roughly **USD 35m in
   a single quarter**. A sum-of-the-parts that adds venture carrying values to a coal
   valuation is adding negatives as positives.

2. **Kideco is a licence-capped, depleting asset.** The IUPK runs to **13 March 2033** —
   about seven years — with a 2022 precedent of the government cutting the concession area
   **28.65%** at renewal. Reserves imply ~17–18 years at current output, so the *licence*
   binds before the geology does. Every EV/EBIT and EPV figure the screener produced treats
   this as a perpetuity.

3. **The coal benchmark is the wrong anchor.** Newcastle at USD 127.85/t (7 Aug 2026) is
   irrelevant to Kideco, which realised **USD 51.1/t in Q1 and USD 54.7/t in H1 2026** —
   roughly 40% of benchmark — because **45% of volume is DMO-capped domestic** against a
   25% national mandate, with Indonesia weighing a rise above 30%. A coal recovery passes
   through weakly and structurally, not cyclically.

**The strongest single cross-check is internal.** The screener's own EPV (2,066) and
Blended IV (2,072) both sit **below** the price of 2,680, while P/B reads 0.65. Cheap on
book, expensive on earnings power. The valuation agent's independent SOTP — built from
scratch on Kideco's licence horizon, Awak Mas at 43% construction and USD 234m sunk of a
USD 429m budget, and ~USD 645m consolidated net debt — landed at a base of **~IDR 1,940**,
close to the screener's number by a completely different method. Two methods agreeing from
different directions is the strongest signal in this file.

**A defect this project introduced was found here and fixed.** When enterprise value was
switched from Yahoo's figure to market cap + net debt, minority interest was dropped —
Yahoo's had included it. EBIT is consolidated, so EV must be too. INDY's EV/EBIT moves
from 9.1x to **10.1x**; 154 of 956 tickers carry minority interest above 5% of market cap.

## 1. Investment classification

**Value trap.** Not a cyclical at a trough, not an asset-value special situation. A
depleting, licence-capped cash engine funding value-destroying ventures, at a discount to
a book value that is itself a wasting asset.

## 2. One-line thesis

> The market is pricing INDY at 0.65x book as though the discount must eventually close,
> but the book is a coal licence expiring in 2033 attached to ventures that burned USD 35m
> in a single quarter — so the discount is compensation for melting ice, not a mispricing.

## 3. Why is it cheap?

- Attributable net profit of ~USD 6.03m in FY2025 — down ~99% from the 2022 peak while
  EBIT fell ~87%, including a Q3 2025 net loss.
- Free cash flow negative, with **99.1% of 1H2026 capex** going into the pre-commercial
  USD 429m Awak Mas gold project.
- **Both agencies have downgraded**: Moody's to B1 (Feb 2026), Fitch to B+ (Apr 2025),
  each citing debt-funded Awak Mas capex against weak coal. Leverage forecast to peak
  ~7.0x in 2026.
- Interest coverage 2.5x into a tightening cycle (BI 5.75%, +100bp since May 2026).

## 4. What is the market missing?

**Probably nothing.** The one genuine candidate is that Petrosea — real, profitable,
third-party contracted, EBITDA margin ~14% guided above 19% for 2026 — is buried inside a
coal holdco and may be undervalued in the consolidated mess. That is an argument for a
break-up, not for owning the holdco.

## 5. What changes?

- **Awak Mas**: trial production Q4 2026, commercial Q1 2027 — EBITDA largely outside a
  12-month window, and it is the *cause* of the leverage, not yet a cure.
- **A Kideco divestment**: the only catalyst that could close the gap. Unconfirmed
  Bloomberg-sourced chatter; management explicitly declined to comment on 17 July 2026.
  Rumour, not a signed deal with disclosed use of proceeds.

## 6–8. Macro / Industry / Micro

**Macro HEADWIND (38/100)** — BI tightening; a weak IDR flatters translated USD earnings,
which is cosmetic. **Industry HEADWIND** — Newcastle softening as India's domestic output
(+7.5% YoY July) displaces imports; DMO reform risk pointing the wrong way, with the March
2026 palm levy increase as precedent that Jakarta will tax commodity upcycles away from
producers. **Micro NEGATIVE** — earnings collapsed, leverage rising, capex pre-commercial.

## 9. Fundamental earnings engine

Kideco tonnes × a DMO-suppressed realised price, less holdco costs and venture losses,
over a licence with seven years to run. Petrosea contributes real service EBITDA. The
frontier ventures subtract.

## 10. Catalyst

**WEAK-TO-MODERATE (42/100)** — and the only large one is a rumour.

## 12. Downside

Genuine impairment risk, not dead money. B1/B+ ratings, leverage peaking ~7.0x, negative
FCF, and a bear case that approaches equity wipeout if the 2033 licence is not renewed on
acceptable terms. This is the first name in this project where permanent capital loss is
the central risk rather than opportunity cost.

## 13. Thesis-breaker (inverted — what would make it investable)

1. A **signed** Kideco transaction at or near USD 1bn with disclosed use of proceeds.
2. Early IUPK renewal to 2040+ without material area reduction.
3. Awak Mas reaching commercial production on budget with positive FCF.
4. A price below ~IDR 1,400, where the base SOTP provides a margin of safety.

## 14. Research gaps

Flagged honestly by the agents rather than guessed: Kideco standalone balance sheet;
goodwill and impairment detail; exact Nusantara ownership percentage; multi-year P/B
history; governance and related-party specifics; IUPK royalty applicability; DMO reform
timing.

## Verdict

**ELIMINATE. The screener's SKIP was correct, and for the right reasons.**

This is the most useful negative result the system has produced, because the bull case was
plausible on its face — a holdco at 0.65x book owning 91% of an asset reportedly worth
more than the entire company. It failed on three independent pieces of evidence, and an
independently-built SOTP agreed with the screener's number by a different route.

**Actionable: no position, and not a watchlist candidate at any price near current levels.**
Revisit only on a *signed* Kideco transaction, or below ~IDR 1,400. Track Awak Mas
commercial production (Q1 2027) as a leverage event, not an earnings event.

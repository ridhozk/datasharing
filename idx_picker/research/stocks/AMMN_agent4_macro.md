## Current Market Narrative

AMMN is priced as a mining-to-refining inflection story: a company that spent 2024-2025 absorbing Phase 8 waste-stripping costs and smelter-commissioning losses is now (2026) showing a real, primary-sourced grade recovery and a smelter that has gone from idle to absorbing 100% of mine concentrate. The stock is extremely volatile and thinly floated (public float only 18.7% per the KBVS shareholder table — three insider blocks hold >68%), which has produced two violent index-driven swings in the past year: an MSCI inclusion rally to the 52-week high (IDR 8,775) and an MSCI Global Standard **deletion** that sent it limit-down to near the 52-week low (IDR 2,710) in May 2026. From that low, AMMN has since rallied hard — IDR 3,150 (2026-06-08, KBVS report price) → ~IDR 4,040 (2026-07-25) → IDR 4,470 (2026-08-11, this brief) — a roughly +42% run in about two months, coincident with the smelter reaching full concentrate absorption and PGT (performance guarantee tests) nearing completion. The screener's SKIP verdict was computed on data as of/around the 2026-03-31 quarter and a lower price; on today's fundamentals the stock has already re-rated a meaningful part of the story described below.

Reasoning: the core operational catalyst — grade recovery at Batu Hijau — is now confirmed with dated, primary-sourced evidence (Cu grade nearly doubled, Au grade more than tripled YoY in Q1 2026), which is a genuinely strong, structural, largely un-hoped-for result. Net: real, dated, partly-delivered catalysts exist, but a meaningful share of the good news looks already priced, and idiosyncratic index/liquidity risk is a genuine, demonstrated tail risk this thesis must carry regardless of operational execution — hence MODERATE, not STRONG.

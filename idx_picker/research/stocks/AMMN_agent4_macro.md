## Current Market Narrative

AMMN is being sold to the market as a **downstreaming/inflection story**: a
low-grade, loss-making transition through late 2025/early 2026 (smelter
ramp-up costs, export-permit gap) giving way to a 2026-2027 step-change in
copper cathode and refined-gold output as Batu Hijau's Phase 8 mines
progressively higher grade ore and the new smelter/precious-metals refinery
(PMR) reaches design capacity. Analyst consensus is bullish (THESIS, sourced
from sell-side, not verified independently — see below) with price targets
well above spot. The screener's SKIP verdict (MOS −100%, PE 41.2x, EV/EBIT
19.1-25.5x depending on measure) says the market has already priced a large
part of this turnaround. The open question for Stage 2 is whether the
remaining catalysts (grade ramp, smelter utilization, Elang) are dated,
quantifiable, and still unpriced, or already fully in the multiple.

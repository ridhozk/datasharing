## The headline: this was my screener's largest false positive

**A 66% margin of safety was a DCF artifact.** The valuation agent read the source,
reproduced my DCF exactly (IV Base 2,736.8), and found two compounding faults:

1. **No working-capital reinvestment term.** The model built free cash flow as
   `normalised EBIT × (1−tax) + depreciation + capex` and grew it at the +16% revenue
   CAGR. For a distributor, growth is funded by **inventory and receivables, not capex**.
   ERAA's working capital runs **9.5% of revenue**; 2025 alone absorbed **IDR 2,775bn**
   while free cash flow was **−680bn**. Adding the term cuts IV Base **2,737 → ~1,617
   (−41%)**.
2. **The terminal value does the rest.** WACC 12% less terminal growth 3% implies an
   **~11x terminal cash-flow multiple driving ~70% of present value**, against the
   **~7.6x EV/EBIT** Indonesian distributors actually trade at.

On peer multiples — the lens that actually fits a thin-margin distributor — **ERAA's own
EV/EBIT of 7.33x is already in line with the peer median of 7.65x.** Fair value lands at
**IDR 350–610**, bracketing both the 470 price and the ~566 sell-side consensus.

Both defects are now **fixed universe-wide**, and the implied terminal multiple is
reported as a column rather than buried.

## Resolving the disagreement

The five verdicts span QUESTIONABLE to CONDITIONAL, with no one calling it a buy. They
agree more than they appear to.

**The −32% FCF yield is real but not what it looks like.** Three of the last four fiscal
years had negative free cash flow — yet **Q2 2026 FCF flipped positive at +IDR 2,331bn**,
and the burn is a disclosed **front-loaded inventory buffer** ahead of the iPhone 17 launch
and further IDR weakness. This is growth working capital, not operational bleeding.

**But growth working capital is only defensible if the returns justify it, and here they
do not.** ROIC is **9.9%** against a cost of capital of 9–12%. Two agents reached this
independently, and it is the single most important economic fact in the file: **ERAA's
growth is value-neutral at best.** My DCF was rewarding +16% growth that earns below its
own cost of capital — the assumption was not merely optimistic, it was **inverted**.

*(This project's model now expresses that case: 4 tickers in the universe are flagged
"Growth destroys value" where the bull IV falls below the bear IV once growth is charged
for the working capital it consumes. ERAA is not among them, but it sits close to the
line.)*

**The 4.7x PE is largely a leverage artifact.** ROE 16.4% against ROIC 9.9%, with **D/E
climbing from ~29% to ~80–99% over five years**. The earnings multiple is low partly
because the equity is geared, not because the business is mispriced.

## What the research cleared, and what it did not

**Cleared — two of my structural worries were unsupported:**
- **Apple is not moving to direct retail in Indonesia.** Its ~USD 1bn commitment is an
  **AirTag plant in Batam** for TKDN compliance, not stores. iBox remains the dominant
  channel with 185 outlets and a 20-year relationship.
- **TKDN is not a threat to ERAA** — it is a *brand-level* burden on Apple and Samsung
  that indirectly favours scaled incumbent distributors. The iPhone ban has been resolved.

**Not cleared:**
- **iBox is not exclusive.** Digimap, Story-i and Blibli also carry Apple. Strong position
  in a contested channel, not a moat.
- **Inventory is the whole risk.** IDR 11,644.7bn at FY2025, **+63% YoY against +17.3%
  revenue growth**; days inventory deteriorated from ~45 to ~59–62; inventory is ~40% of
  total assets and **1.7x the entire market cap**. Payables cover only ~51% of it — the
  rest is short-term bank debt, and **quarterly debt tracks the inventory cycle almost
  exactly**. Quick ratio of 0.40 means liquidity is entirely inventory-dependent.
- **A dated near-term item:** an **SGD 50mn subsidiary bond matures ~24 August 2026** —
  about two weeks from this review — with refinancing terms unconfirmed.

## Macro is a confirmed headwind

Consumer confidence at a **nine-month low of 117.8**; retail sales **−3.9% / −3.7% YoY**
through Apr–Jun 2026; BI +100bp since May; new OJK BNPL and consumer-credit restrictions
tightening through 2026 — material because much of Indonesian handset demand is financed.
And the **IDR is down ~10% over twelve months** against an ~11% gross margin, with
management acknowledging FX pressure "across almost all business lines."

## Verdict

**ELIMINATE from the portfolio. Downgraded from BUY.**

Not because the business is failing — it is not. Revenue grows, Q1 2026 profit rose 133%,
Q2 free cash flow turned positive, the Apple relationship is intact and the TKDN ban is
resolved. The Thesis Killer's **CONDITIONAL** rather than REJECT is the fair reading of the
company.

It is eliminated because **the reason it appeared on the shortlist does not survive
scrutiny.** The 66% margin of safety came from a DCF missing a working-capital term and
carrying an ~11x terminal multiple. Corrected, ERAA trades **in line with its peers** —
and the growth that made it look cheap earns **below its cost of capital**, so the growth
is not worth paying for.

**What would change it:** ROIC sustainably above ~12% (most plausibly through the
margin-accretive ERAL lifestyle mix-shift reaching 25–30% of revenue), inventory days back
under ~45 with the bond refinanced, or a price near **IDR 350** where the peer-multiple
bear case provides genuine downside protection.

**Actionable: no position. Alert at IDR 350. Watch the 24 August bond refinancing** — a
failure or punitive repricing there would move this from "fairly valued" to a
balance-sheet story quickly.

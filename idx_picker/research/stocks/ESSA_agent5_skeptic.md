## Priority 1 — Is the free cash flow already spoken for?

### Growth capex — the hypothesised kill FAILS. I am killing my own kill.

[FACT] ESSA's 2026 capex budget is **USD 20 million (~IDR 332 bn)**. CFO Prakash Chand Bumb described it as *"essential"* rather than expansionary, supporting existing LPG and ammonia facilities, and said downstream projects "are still in early development stages" and will not impose significant capital expenditure burdens in 2026. ([Infobanknews](https://infobanknews.com/essa-fokus-operasional-lpg-dan-amoniak-di-2026-siapkan-capex-segini/))

[FACT] Corroborated by reported figures: TTM capex IDR 117 bn; FY2025 IDR 141.7 bn; capex never exceeded IDR 142 bn in any of FY2022-2025.

[FACT] Moreover the company is *cancelling* growth spending, not committing it: the **Sustainable Aviation Fuel project was postponed on 27 July 2026**, framed by management as capital preservation. ([Mikirduit](https://www.mikirduit.com/laba-saham-essa-naik-103-persen-tapi-harga-saham-loyo-begini-penjelasannya/))

[INFERENCE] So the brief's hypothesis — *cash committed to a second ammonia plant / soda ash project, therefore the FCF yield is illusory and the dividend unsafe* — **does not hold.** The soda ash and downstream ambitions are pre-FID. I want this on the record because a Thesis Killer that only ever confirms the kill it was sent to find is not doing its job.

[THESIS] The finding inverts into a different concern. A company spending USD 20 m on an IDR 11.6 tn asset base, cancelling its flagship growth project, and paying out 125-135% of earnings is not compounding — it is **harvesting a single asset and distributing the proceeds**. That is a defensible capital-allocation choice; it is not a business that deserves a growth multiple, and it caps the terminal value at whatever the Banggai plant is worth.

### The dividend — this is what the cash was actually spoken for

[FACT] At the AGM of **18 June 2026**, ESSA declared **IDR 895.8 bn (IDR 52/share)**, equal to **125.59% of FY2025 net profit of USD 40.29 m**, with approximately **IDR 182.65 bn drawn from retained earnings**. Cum date 26 June 2026. Prior-year (FY2024) payout was **~23%** — a five-fold increase in the per-share dividend. ([EmitenNews](https://emitennews.com/news/essa-jadwal-dividen-rp8958-miliar-cum-date-26-juni-2026), [PintarSaham](https://pintarsaham.id/dividen-essa-2026-essa-bagikan-dividen-tunai-rp89580-miliar-atau-rp52-per-saham/), [Stockbit Snips](https://snips.stockbit.com/stockbit-research/essa-akan-bagikan-dividen-dengan-indikasi-yield-81))

[FACT] A second source puts it at **135% of 2025 earnings** (USD ~50.5 m distributed). ([Saham Daily](https://www.sahamdaily.com/essa-update-24-juli-2026/)) **Range: 125-135%. Either way it exceeds 100%.**

[FACT — internal cross-check, requires no external source] The fact sheet refutes itself on its own arithmetic:
`Payout = Dividend Yield × PE = 0.0819 × 11.982 = 0.981`
A ~98% payout on TTM earnings cannot coexist with the reported `Payout Ratio 0.2023`. **The screener's 0.2023 is a stale FY2024 field.** Anyone reading the fact sheet concludes the dividend is covered ~5x. It is not covered at all. The brief's lead is confirmed.

[THESIS] **The 8% yield is in part a return *of* capital.** IDR 182.65 bn of it is a distribution from the balance sheet — a partial liquidation of the IDR 2,944 bn net cash position. An investor extrapolating 8% is extrapolating a payout ratio management has never committed to.

[FACT] Management's own language on the dividend: it "depends on shareholder approval," and they "hope to continue distributing dividends as previously done." That is **not a payout policy.** ([Infobanknews](https://infobanknews.com/essa-fokus-operasional-lpg-dan-amoniak-di-2026-siapkan-capex-segini/))

[FACT] Mitigant: 2026 was the **fifth consecutive year** of dividends. The habit is real; only the magnitude is unrepeatable.

[INFERENCE] If ESSA reverts to a 40-50% payout on mid-cycle earnings, the yield is **~3-4%**, not 8%. The single most-quoted reason to own this stock is the least reliable number in the file.

### Is the 20% FCF yield real? Probably not.

[INFERENCE — data-integrity flag, moderate confidence] The reported TTM FCF of IDR 2,283 bn is likely **overstated**:
- `Operating Cash Flow (TTM)` is blank. Per CLAUDE.md, Yahoo publishes no `annualOperatingCashFlow` for IDX; FCF here is Yahoo's own unverified number.

[ASSUMPTION] At IDR ~500 bn of true D&A and honest maintenance capex, FCF is nearer IDR 1.3-1.5 tn — an **11-13% FCF yield, not 20%**. Still good; but the headline inflates the Value Score of 83.8.

([Mikirduit](https://www.mikirduit.com/prospek-saham-essa-saat-harga-ammonia-tinggi-dan-harga-saham-lagi-dibanting/))

[FACT] **ICIS, 28 April 2026: "Global ammonia prices to stay elevated in H2 '26; to fall from Jan '27."** ([ICIS](https://www.icis.com/explore/resources/news/2026/04/28/11201985/insight-global-ammonia-prices-to-stay-elevated-in-h2-26-to-fall-from-jan-27))

[INFERENCE] Realised prices are up 70% YoY, benchmark is at a three-year high, the driver is a *geopolitical supply disruption* rather than structural demand, and the consensus forecast calls for reversion from January 2027. **These are peak-of-cycle conditions.** A realised ASP +70% YoY is not a new normal.

### But the gas cost moves WITH ammonia — the honest correction to the brief

[FACT] The 2014 PJBG between JOB Pertamina-Medco Tomori Sulawesi (JOBPMTS) and PT Panca Amara Utama supplies **55 MMSCFD**, and **the agreed gas price references international ammonia prices** (originally USD 8.44/MMBTU struck against ~USD 720/t ammonia). ([Dunia Energi](https://www.dunia-energi.com/job-pertamina-medco-tomori-pasok-gas-untuk-panca-amara-utama/), [Bisnis](https://market.bisnis.com/read/20140314/192/210833/job-medco-teken-pjbg-dengan-panca-amara-utama))

[FACT] Confirmed as still operative: ESSA and Medco use *"a progressive gas pricing formula following global ammonia price fluctuations,"* which **stabilises margins for both parties** and provides a *"margin cushion."* ([Mikirduit](https://www.mikirduit.com/prospek-saham-essa-saat-harga-ammonia-tinggi-dan-harga-saham-lagi-dibanting/))

[INFERENCE] **This materially weakens the pure "peak spread collapses" bear case, and I must say so.** If ammonia falls, gas cost falls with it. ESSA is less exposed to the ammonia *level* than a fixed-cost converter would be. Part of the H1-2026 margin gain therefore came from **volume and utilisation** (production +16% YoY, utilisation 121% of nameplate) rather than pure spread.

[THESIS] **The correct reformulation of the risk is therefore not "ammonia falls" but "the indexation disappears."** The margin cushion is a *contractual* feature, not a physical one — and that contract expires in December 2027.

### The screener's normalisation is still broken

[FACT] `Normalised EBIT (5Y) = IDR 1,424 bn` is the median of only **four** annual observations (5,403.8 / 1,379.7 / 1,468.6 / 1,267.5) — `Years of Annual History: 4`, despite the "5Y" label.

[FACT] `Normalised/Trailing EBIT = 0.852` ⟹ trailing EBIT ≈ **IDR 1,671 bn**, **32% above FY2025** and 17% above a four-year median that itself contains the 2022 supercycle.

[INFERENCE] The screener values ESSA on a run-rate **above its own mid-cycle estimate**, where that estimate is contaminated upward. Both errors point the same way: **IV is too high.** Normalise to the 2023-2025 median (IDR 1,379.7 bn) and the earnings base falls 17% — enough on its own to take a 10% MOS negative.

[FACT] **Utilisation is already 121% of nameplate.** ([Saham Daily](https://www.sahamdaily.com/essa-update-24-juli-2026/)) [INFERENCE] There is no volume headroom left. Any further earnings growth must come from price, in a market forecast to fall from January 2027.

## The 2027 gas contract cliff — the real thesis-breaker

This deserves its own section because it is the finding that decides the verdict.

[FACT] PAU receives 55 MMSCFD from the Senoro block **until December 2027**. The original PJBG (13 March 2014) ran 2015-2027, total volume 248,200 MMSCF, contract value ~USD 1.4 bn.

[FACT] **The Senoro-Toili block contract is extended for 20 years effective 4 December 2027**, with participating interests changing to **PT Pertamina Hulu Energi Tomori Sulawesi 50% (operator)**, PT Medco E&P Tomori Sulawesi 30%, Tomori E&P Limited 20%. ([CNN Indonesia](https://www.cnnindonesia.com/ekonomi/20211129175109-85-727540/kontrak-blok-senoro-toili-pertamina-medco-diperpanjang-hingga-20-tahun), [Kontan](https://industri.kontan.co.id/news/medco-ep-raih-perpanjangan-kontrak-blok-senoro-toili-untuk-20-tahun))

[FACT] **Negotiations over the supply extension and a new price formula — which will determine ESSA's post-2027 production cost structure — are ongoing.** Phase 2 of Senoro-Toili is earmarked to supply DSLNG, PAU's ammonia plant, PLN and Jargas. ([Kabar Luwuk](https://kabarluwuk.com/perpanjang-kontrak-blok-senoro-toili-fase-2-bisnis-berkelanjutan-medcoenergi/))

[FACT] **Gas is 88.39% of ESSA's total raw-material expense (USD 65.09 m figure cited).** Gas is simultaneously the feedstock and the energy source. Analysts identify the renegotiation as the **primary concern** and note an unfavourable formula "could substantially compress margins." ([Mikirduit](https://www.mikirduit.com/laba-saham-essa-naik-103-persen-tapi-harga-saham-loyo-begini-penjelasannya/))

**Why this is the kill:**

[THESIS] Four risks converge on one date:
1. **Single supplier.** One consortium, one pipeline, one block. No alternative feedstock exists for a plant in Banggai, Central Sulawesi.
2. **88% of raw-material cost.** There is no other cost line to flex in compensation.
3. **The counterparty changes.** Pertamina moves to 50% and operatorship from 4 December 2027. [ASSUMPTION — labelled speculation] A state-owned operator negotiating domestic gas allocation faces political and fiscal incentives — domestic gas obligations, government take, competing claims from PLN and Jargas on the same Phase 2 volumes — that a private seller does not. There is no reason to assume the 2014 terms carry over.
4. **The ammonia indexation itself is the thing being renegotiated.** The very feature that makes ESSA's margin defensible is a clause in an expiring contract.

[INFERENCE] An investor buying at IDR 655 with a 10% margin of safety is underwriting an **unquantifiable binary event 16 months out that resets 88% of the cost base.** You cannot take binary contract risk of that magnitude on a 10% cushion. The correct MOS demand for an unresolved input-cost reset of this size is 40%+, not 10%.

### The USD-reporting / IDR-trading mismatch under BI tightening

[FACT] ESSA reports in **USD**; the stock trades in **IDR**; the screener applied FX 17,908. Revenue (ammonia, LPG) is USD-priced; gas cost is USD-priced. The economics are dollar economics.

[INFERENCE] A **stronger IDR is directly negative** for the translated numbers. If USD/IDR falls from 17,908 to 16,500 (−7.9%), an unchanged USD P&L translates to ~7.9% lower IDR net income and ~7.9% lower IDR book value — and, mechanically, ~7.9% lower IDR intrinsic value, because the screener builds IV from FX-converted statements. **A 10% MOS is entirely consumed by a 10% IDR appreciation.**

[FACT + INFERENCE] This is live. **Bank Indonesia has tightened from 4.75% to 5.75% since May 2026 with a hike bias.** Tightening is IDR-supportive. The prevailing macro regime therefore (a) erodes translated earnings and (b) raises the IDR discount rate applied to them. Both push IV down simultaneously. The screener models neither.

[INFERENCE] Compounding this: reported **Beta is 0.206**, far below the CLAUDE.md trust band of [0.5, 2.5]. For a single-asset commodity converter with a binary contract event pending, a beta of 0.206 is not merely untrustworthy — it is absurd. Any residual under-estimate of cost of equity inflates the EPV (708) and IV Base (679).

## The value trap test — why has the market NOT recognised this?

Concretely, and I think the market is right:

1. **The market is refusing to capitalise a cost structure that expires.** [THESIS — primary explanation] Analysts covering the name identify the 2027 gas renegotiation as the *primary concern* by name. This is not an overlooked stock; it is a **correctly discounted** one. The discount is the market pricing an unresolved binary.
2. **Price fell while earnings doubled.** The stock is **−34% from its 52-week high of 995** while H1 net profit rose 103%. That is a multiple contraction deliberately applied to earnings the market does not believe are durable. The screener sees "cheap on trailing earnings"; the market sees "trailing earnings are the problem."
3. **The 8% yield is publicly known to be a 125-135% payout.** Stockbit, EmitenNews, PintarSaham and Saham Daily all published the figure. Indonesian retail — who dominate this counter — can read. The yield is not mispriced; it is *discounted* because it is known to be unrepeatable.
4. **The growth story keeps being cancelled.** Revenue CAGR 3Y is **−26.1%**. SAF was suspended on 27 July 2026. Blue-ammonia premium (11-14%, targeting 20%) does not arrive until **late 2027-2028**. Soda ash remains "early development." A story that stays permanently two years away is priced as a story that never arrives.
5. **One plant, one supplier, one customer** structurally deserves a discount, and no catalyst changes any of the three.

**This is a textbook value-trap profile**: fortress balance sheet, high headline yield, cheap trailing multiples, shrinking top line, single asset, commodity exposure, and a cost base with a known expiry date. Per CLAUDE.md, **cheapness alone never produces a BUY** — and cheapness is essentially the entire case (Value Score 83.8 vs Quality Score 61.7).

Layered on top: **~90% of Q1 revenue came from one customer** (Genesis Corporation, USD 85.58 m of USD 95 m); the dividend anchoring the 8% yield was paid at **125-135% of FY2025 earnings** with IDR 182.65 bn taken from retained earnings; utilisation is already at **121% of nameplate** so no volume growth remains; the SAF growth project was **suspended on 27 July 2026**; and the screener's own `Normalised/Trailing EBIT` of 0.852 concedes trailing EBIT sits 17% above a mid-cycle estimate contaminated by the 2022 supercycle and computed from only four annual observations. Correct the earnings base, add back the ~IDR 896 bn dividend the stale 31-March balance sheet still counts as cash, and EV/EBIT is **8.6x, not 6.5x** — the 10% MOS is gone before any bear assumption is applied.

**Why The Stock Could Be A Value Trap:**
The stock is **34% below its 52-week high while half-year profit doubled** — price falling as earnings rise is a market deliberately de-rating earnings it does not believe survive 2027, not a market overlooking value. Revenue has compounded at **−26.1% for three years**, the SAF project is cancelled, blue ammonia does not arrive until late 2027-2028, and soda ash remains "early development" behind USD 20 m of "essential" capex — so there is no growth to wait for. What remains is a shrinking, single-site, single-supplier, single-customer converter whose cheapness exists only while ammonia is at a three-year high and whose yield exists only while the balance sheet is being paid down. Nothing on the horizon changes the asset count, the feedstock dependency, or the customer concentration — which are the three reasons the discount exists in the first place.

**Biggest Hidden Risk:**
**Customer concentration.** The gas cliff is at least publicly discussed; the offtake is not. A single buyer, Genesis Corporation, accounted for **USD 85.58 m against USD 95 m of total Q1 2026 revenue**. Nothing in the screener flags it (`Red Flags` is blank), no Stage-2 lens has priced it, and I could not establish the contract's tenor, pricing basis, or whether it is arm's-length. Loss or repricing of that single relationship would be as damaging as the gas renewal and would arrive with far less warning. Runner-up hidden risk: the **USD-reporting / IDR-trading mismatch under a tightening Bank Indonesia (4.75% → 5.75% since May 2026, hike-biased)** — an IDR-supportive regime shrinks translated IDR earnings and book value roughly one-for-one while raising the domestic discount rate, so a 10% IDR appreciation alone erases the entire 10% margin of safety. The screener models neither.

**Most Fragile Assumption:**
That the ammonia-linked gas pricing formula survives the December 2027 renewal intact. Every bull argument — the margin cushion, the ~35% operating margin, the EPV of 708, the durability of the dividend — rests on a clause in a contract that expires in 16 months and is being renegotiated against a counterparty consortium whose operator is about to change to a state-owned company with competing domestic allocation obligations to PLN, Jargas and DSLNG from the same Phase 2 volumes. There is no disclosed successor formula, and no basis for assuming the 2014 terms roll over.

**Most Important Thesis-Breaker:**
A post-2027 gas price formula that raises the floor or weakens the ammonia linkage, landing simultaneously with the ICIS-forecast ammonia decline from January 2027. Revenue back to the FY2025 level of IDR 5,283 bn at a 20% operating margin gives EBIT ~IDR 1,060 bn and net income ~IDR 620 bn; at the peer 6.55x EV/EBIT plus post-dividend net cash that is **~IDR 522/share, −20% from here**, with the dividend cut ~65% to IDR 18/share. The 52-week low of 505 shows the market has already priced that scenario once within the past year. Because volume is capped at 121% utilisation, there is no operational lever to offset it.

**What Evidence Would Change My Mind:**
1. **A signed post-2027 gas supply agreement with disclosed pricing.** If the ammonia indexation is preserved on comparable terms with a tenor beyond 2035, my central objection collapses and this becomes a genuine BUY candidate at a modest discount. This single disclosure is worth more than everything else on this list combined.
2. **Offtake disclosure on Genesis Corporation** — tenor, pricing mechanism, arm's-length status, and the share of revenue at risk. Evidence of a diversified or contractually locked customer base would remove the biggest hidden risk.
3. **A stated dividend policy expressed as a percentage of earnings**, replacing "depends on shareholder approval." A committed 50-60% policy on mid-cycle earnings would make the yield analysable instead of extrapolated.
4. **The audited FY2025 / H1-2026 cash flow statement**, to test whether IDR 2,283 bn TTM FCF and the implausible IDR 53.9 bn of implied D&A are real. Genuine, repeatable 2.4x FCF conversion would make the FCF-yield case far stronger than I have allowed.
5. **Evidence that ammonia tightness is structural** — permanent European or Chinese capacity closure, or a durable Indonesian gas-cost advantage — rather than a Middle East supply disruption that reverses.
6. **FID with committed financing on soda ash or blue ammonia at a credible IRR.** Counter-intuitively this would *improve* my view: it would replace a harvest-one-asset profile with a reinvestment runway — provided it is not funded by cutting the dividend investors are actually buying.

**Probability Thesis Is Fundamentally Correct: 40%**
Meaning: ~40% probability that ESSA at IDR 655 delivers an adequate risk-adjusted return over 3-5 years. The balance sheet makes catastrophic loss unlikely — I put permanent impairment below 10%. But the modal outcome is a de-rating toward IDR 520-600 as ammonia reverts, the payout resets and the gas formula is repriced: dead money with a shrinking dividend. Roughly 35-40% of the probability mass sits on a favourable gas renewal plus sustained ammonia strength, in which case the stock is worth 800-900. That is a coin flip on a disclosure nobody has, and a coin flip is not an investment case at a 10% margin of safety.

**Investment Committee Verdict: REJECT**

I am rejecting **at IDR 655**, not rejecting the company. ESSA is genuinely well-capitalised, genuinely low-cost, genuinely shareholder-friendly, and I would revisit it without prejudice.

But the screener's MOS of 10% is already below the 30% buy threshold, and I find **three independent corrections that each push intrinsic value down before any bear assumption is applied**: normalising EBIT to the 2023-2025 median cuts the earnings base ~17%; the ~IDR 896 bn dividend paid after the stale 31-March balance sheet raises EV ~8%; and IDR appreciation under BI tightening reduces translated IDR earnings roughly one-for-one. Any single one takes the margin of safety through zero. **When the arithmetic corrections alone are larger than the margin of safety, the answer is no** — and that is before the December 2027 gas cliff, which is an unquantifiable binary reset of 88% of the cost base that no 10% cushion can absorb.

Per the framework's five outcomes, ESSA classifies as **"fairly valued" trending toward "value trap."** Not "average business very cheap" — the cheapness is an artefact of peak-price trailing earnings and an overstated FCF figure. Not "deep value" — the asset is one operating plant, not a discount to hard asset value (NCAV IDR 180 vs price 655; `Net-Net Pass: False`).

**I am exercising veto power. ESSA does not proceed to the Top 10 or the IDR 100 m portfolio at IDR 655.**

Reconsider on **either** trigger, whichever comes first:
- **Price at or below IDR 500**, which restores a ~30% MOS against a normalised IV of ~700 and prices the contract risk explicitly; **or**
- **Disclosure of the post-2027 gas supply agreement on terms preserving the ammonia indexation**, at which point the name should be re-underwritten from scratch at whatever price then prevails — including above 655.

## The one thing to read first

**FACT (primary source — company disclosure, essa.id, gas HoA signed 2013-01-21).** PT Panca
Amara Utama's gas supply from JOB Pertamina-Medco E&P Tomori Sulawesi (Senoro field) is
**55 MMSCFD, running through December 2027, at a price contractually indexed to the ammonia
price index.** The reference point disclosed at signing: at ammonia USD 720/MT, gas cost
USD 8.44/MMBTU.

**This changes the entire shape of the investment and neither the screener nor the popular
narrative reflects it:**

1. **ESSA is not a fixed-cost commodity producer with leveraged exposure to ammonia.** Its
   principal input cost *rises and falls with its output price by contract*. The spread is
   **partially self-hedging**. Gas is **88.39% of raw material cost (USD 65.09m in H1 2026)**.
2. **Upside is muted:** a +70% realised price move produced only a 27.7% → 35.2% gross margin
   expansion, because the gas bill rose with it. Anyone modelling ESSA as
   "ammonia price × 720kt − fixed cost" will badly overestimate the earnings beta.
3. **Downside is cushioned:** this is why ESSA stayed comfortably profitable through the
   2023–2025 ammonia depression (revenue CAGR −26%) with an F-Score of 7. It is a materially
   better-quality cyclical than the screener's "Business Type: Cyclical" tag implies.
4. **There is a hard-dated cliff: December 2027.** The contract expires in ~16 months. The
   renegotiation is targeted for completion **by end-2026 — inside the 12-month window.** This
   is the real catalyst, it is binary, and its sign is unknown.

the **suspension of the SAF project**, which removed the long-term growth driver. The market

**INFERENCE.** The market has already decided what ESSA is: a **terminal-value-free cash

being priced as a dividend stream of uncertain duration, not as an earnings recovery. The
32% discount to the historical P/E is not an anomaly the market has missed — it is the
market's judgement on peak earnings and contract risk.

## THESIS vs CATALYST — kept strictly separate

### THESIS (why it may be worth more than 655)
**THESIS.** ESSA is a debt-free, single-asset ammonia producer whose feedstock is contractually
indexed to its output price — a **structurally self-hedging spread** that has kept it profitable
through a brutal three-year ammonia downcycle. It runs at 100%+ utilisation, converts more than
20% of market cap to free cash flow, has suspended all discretionary capex, and distributes
essentially everything. At 11.2x earnings and 1.38x book it trades ~30% below its own five-year
averages, with a fortress balance sheet (Altman Z 11.3, current ratio 11.5x, interest coverage
66x). Screener blended IV 732 vs price 655 → MOS ~10%.

**The thesis rests on two assumptions, and both must hold:**
- **ASSUMPTION 1:** ammonia mid-cycle is nearer USD 500/MT than the USD 350–400/MT of
  2023–2025. Currently unproven — Tampa is at USD 487 and falling.
- **ASSUMPTION 2:** the December 2027 gas contract is renewed on terms that preserve ammonia
  indexation and volume. **Unknown. This is the terminal value.**

If Assumption 2 fails, the self-hedge disappears and ESSA becomes an ordinary levered-to-price
cyclical — a completely different, and much worse, security.

### CATALYST (what re-prices it inside 12 months)
The price catalyst **fired in H1 2026 and has been reported**. The growth catalyst **was
suspended and the market already sold it**. The volume catalyst is **consensus**. What remains
is one binary event with an unknown sign.

**1. Gas contract renegotiation concluding by end-2026 — DATED, BINARY, MATERIAL, SIGN UNKNOWN**

## Catalyst Verdict: **WEAK**

**Reasoning.** The catalyst that mattered — the ammonia price — fired in H1 2026, has been
reported, and is now reversing: Tampa settled at roughly USD 487/MT in August, below ESSA's own
H1 realised USD 531, with the Hormuz disruption that created the spike being negotiated away
and ~113 new ammonia plants due by 2028. The growth catalyst was suspended, and the market has
already marked the stock down for it — Rp 655 to Rp 610 while profit doubled, which tells you
the market is pricing this deliberately, not overlooking it. The volume recovery is real and
dated but sits inside consensus, so it can only disappoint. The dividend is projected flat to
−19% off an unsustainable 135% payout. Blue ammonia is a feasibility study waiting on the IMO.

**Plainly: there is no credible, dated, positive, not-already-priced catalyst inside 12
months.** The one genuinely live and unpriced item — the gas contract renegotiation targeted
for end-2026 — is **binary with an unknown sign**, and is as likely to be a negative catalyst
as a positive one. That is a finding, not a research gap.

**This does not make ESSA uninvestable — and the gas indexation makes it a better business than
the screener's "Cyclical" tag suggests.** A producer whose dominant input cost moves with its
output price has structurally compressed cyclicality in both directions, which is exactly what
the 2023–2025 survival record and the F-Score of 7 demonstrate. Combined with net cash, Altman
Z of 11.3, a >20% FCF yield, and management choosing distribution over empire-building at a
cyclical peak, this is a genuinely well-run, well-financed asset.

**But it must be underwritten as what it is:** a single-asset cash-return cyclical bought on
valuation and balance sheet, held through the cycle, with the dividend as the return
mechanism — **not as a catalyst trade, and not on a trailing 8% yield that has already been
paid.** The screener's 10% MOS is thin compensation for a commodity producer whose realised
price just rose 70% on a geopolitical risk premium that is currently unwinding.

**The two questions that decide this name — in order:**
1. **Is the December 2027 gas contract renewed with ammonia indexation intact?** This is the
   terminal value, and it is not in any published model I found.
2. **Is USD 500+/MT ammonia mid-cycle, or was it Hormuz?** The August Tampa print of ~USD 487
   is the early evidence, and it argues Hormuz.

Everything else is second-order.
